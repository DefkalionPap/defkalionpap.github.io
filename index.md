Hello, I’m Defkalion, a freelance VR and game developer based in Thessaloniki, Greece. I hold an Integrated Master’s degree from the Film School at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (AUTh), where I studied film and virtual-augmented-mixed reality. I graduated with a score of 9.15/10 (“Excellent”). I’m also certified in UX Design by Google and Game Design by CalArts.

During my studies, I interned at the Centre for Research and Technology Hellas (CERTH) as an XR Tool Developer and at Onassis General High School of Thessaloniki as an XR Cinema Educator. I also volunteered as a film critic for the 2025 Reykjavik International Film Festival.

My professional and research interests include virtual reality, user experience design, game design and development. I have experience creating levels in Unity and Unreal Engine, scripting in C# for Unity, and building gameplay systems with Unreal Engine Blueprints. I also make prototypes using Figma and Max/MSP.

I’m authorized to work throughout the European Union and the United States.
