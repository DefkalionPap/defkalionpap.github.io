As an XR Cinema Educator at the Onassis High School of Thessaloniki, I guided students through an introduction to XR, VR and AR, exploring how sound and music shape film, developing screenwriting skills, and prototyping with Unity and Assemblr Studio. In the second half of the course, students and a collaborative team—including a film-school student and two teachers—worked together to create an original VR film in Unity. We brought the project to life with student-inspired 3D avatars made in Avaturn, music composed using a free online tool, a script developed in Google Docs, and environments built with free Sketchfab models and Mixamo animations. The film was screened at the student film festival «Μύρισε Σινεμά».
